# Resource Navigation Structure & Icons

Based on the school/education management system, here are the navigation groups and appropriate Heroicons for each resource:

## Navigation Groups

### Academic & Report Resources
- **AcademicReportResource**: `heroicon-o-document-chart-bar` or `heroicon-o-clipboard-document-list`
- **AcademicYears**: `heroicon-o-calendar-days` or `heroicon-o-calendar`

### Student & Attendance Management
- **Students**: `heroicon-o-users` or `heroicon-o-user-group`
- **Attendances**: `heroicon-o-check-circle` or `heroicon-o-calendar-days`
- **StudentProgress**: `heroicon-o-chart-bar-square` or `heroicon-o-trending-up`

### Library Management
- **LibraryBooks**: `heroicon-o-book-open` or `heroicon-o-book-shelf`
- **BookCategories**: `heroicon-o-tag` or `heroicon-o-folder`
- **BookIssues**: `heroicon-o-arrow-right-circle` or `heroicon-o-hand-raised`
- **BookReservations**: `heroicon-o-bookmark` or `heroicon-o-clock`
- **LibraryFines**: `heroicon-o-currency-dollar` or `heroicon-o-exclamation-triangle`

### Examination System
- **Exams**: `heroicon-o-clipboard-document-check` or `heroicon-o-document-text`
- **ExamMarks**: `heroicon-o-star` or `heroicon-o-trophy`
- **ExamSubjects**: `heroicon-o-academic-cap` or `heroicon-o-squares-plus`

### Academic Structure
- **Schools**: `heroicon-o-building-office-2` or `heroicon-o-home-modern`
- **SchoolClasses**: `heroicon-o-users` or `heroicon-o-rectangle-group`
- **Subjects**: `heroicon-o-book-open` or `heroicon-o-squares-2x2`
- **Teachers**: `heroicon-o-user-circle` or `heroicon-o-identification`
- **Staff**: `heroicon-o-user-group` or `heroicon-o-briefcase`

### Schedule Management
- **Timetables**: `heroicon-o-table-cells` or `heroicon-o-calendar`
- **Periods**: `heroicon-o-clock` or `heroicon-o-squares-2x2`

## Implementation Notes

Each resource should include:
1. Proper navigation group assignment
2. Appropriate Heroicon
3. Navigation badge with relevant count
4. Consistent color scheme
5. Logical navigation sort order within groups
